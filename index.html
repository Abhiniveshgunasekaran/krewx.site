About KrewX

Built for the ride, not just the chat.

KrewX is a mobile platform built for motorcycle rider communities. It brings group communication, ride planning, live convoy tracking, emergency SOS alerts, route sharing and verified rider profiles and clubs together in one app.

Unlike general messaging apps, KrewX is ride-aware. Its communication, location and safety features are designed around the ride itself, before, during and after, and tuned for riders on Indian roads.

One app for your whole krew

Solo riders, riding groups and clubs: plan together, ride together, stay safe together.

The problem

Group rides are still coordinated on scattered tools.

Motorcycle riding groups in India plan rides across WhatsApp groups, phone calls and separate map apps. Nothing is built for what actually happens on the road.

Scattered coordination

Plans, routes and updates are split across multiple chats, calls and map apps.

Riders get separated

During group rides there's no easy way to see where everyone in the convoy is.

No fast emergency alert

When something goes wrong, there's no quick way to alert the whole group with your location.

No trusted platform

Rider communities lack a verified, structured place for planning rides, safety and knowledge sharing.

What's in KrewX

Everything your krew needs, in one app.

From the first message to the last pit stop, KrewX keeps your group connected and safe.

Ride planning

Create group rides with meet-up points, pit stops and destinations. Members request to join, and you manage the roster.

Live convoy tracking

See every rider in your group on a live map during the ride, so no one gets left behind.

Emergency SOS

Raise an SOS alert to your ride group with your GPS location in seconds. Your marker stands out on the map.

Group communication

Ride chats with photos and voice notes, plus club announcements, all tied to the ride or club they belong to.

Radar

Discover nearby riders, open rides, fuel stops and garages around you.

Verified riders & clubs

Verified rider profiles with your garage of bikes, and structured clubs with members, events and admin controls.

Events

Clubs can create events, take registrations and keep a history of every ride and event.

Revvit

A community feed to share routes, destinations, tips and stories with riders everywhere.
